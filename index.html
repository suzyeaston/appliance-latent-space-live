02 / TEACH THE LIGHT

A visual language, taught by you.

0 taught · 0 trained

While a loop plays, design its look. Give that pairing a name and teach it. Change the music or tone controls and repeat with a different look. Three distinct examples unlock your model.

Your library & how learning works

This is local example-based regression, not a pretrained image generator. It learns a mapping between musical features (pitch, density, velocity, duration, percussion, tempo and tone settings) and the looks you choose. The drawing system is procedural; your examples teach its parameters. No examples are bundled and nothing is uploaded. Every visitor has a separate local library.

Export this library separately from your music project. Browser storage can be cleared or separated between the embedded and full-page versions. Your sound is still synthesized; a model trained on your recordings is a future stage.
